# REALISTIC FASHION PRESENTATION ANIMATION

## OBJECTIVE

Animate the subject from the input image as a real person naturally presenting her outfit in a realistic POV video.

Preserve the subject's identity exactly.

The animation should resemble an authentic smartphone recording rather than a staged fashion shoot.

---

# IDENTITY LOCK

Preserve exactly:

* Face
* Facial identity
* Expression structure
* Hair
* Skin tone
* Skin texture
* Makeup
* Body proportions
* Pose proportions
* Clothing
* Accessories

Do not beautify, redesign, stylize, or reinterpret any aspect of the subject.

Identity preservation has absolute priority.

---

# CAMERA

POV perspective.

The camera remains completely stationary throughout the video.

Maintain:

* Eye-to-chest height
* Fixed framing
* Fixed focal length
* No zoom
* No pan
* No tilt
* No shake
* No handheld jitter
* No focus breathing

The subject moves naturally within the frame while the camera never moves.

---

# ANIMATION SEQUENCE

The subject begins standing naturally at a comfortable distance from the camera.

She calmly walks forward with two small, relaxed steps.

While approaching, she gently raises one hand and lightly touches the bow or lace detail located on the chest area.

The gesture is subtle and effortless, as if casually showing a clothing detail.

She briefly holds the fabric before naturally releasing it.

She then takes one small step back, returning to approximately her original position.

The movement flows continuously without pauses or abrupt transitions.

---

# BODY LANGUAGE

Natural, relaxed posture.

Shoulders remain loose.

One hand naturally rests near the waist whenever appropriate.

Weight shifts subtly between both legs.

Small natural hip movement.

Gentle torso rotation to reveal the clothing.

Relaxed breathing throughout the animation.

Natural balance adjustments.

No rigid posture.

---

# CLOTHING PRESENTATION

The clothing remains the focus.

Movements should naturally reveal:

* Fit
* Fabric
* Shape
* Texture
* Movement

Avoid exaggerated modeling behavior.

No runway walking.

No influencer poses.

No dramatic gestures.

The presentation should feel effortless and spontaneous.

---

# FACIAL PERFORMANCE

Expressions change naturally throughout the animation.

The subject never maintains the same expression continuously.

Sequence:

Approaching:

* Neutral expression
* Relaxed lips
* Calm eyes
* Comfortable confidence

Touching the bow:

* A brief, genuine smile
* Slight cheek movement
* Soft eye expression

After releasing the fabric:

* Expression naturally returns to neutral

Later:

* Small head turn
* Brief warm smile
* Smile fades naturally

Expressions should appear organically rather than being deliberately posed.

Avoid:

* Frozen smile
* Constant grin
* Exaggerated expressions
* Overacting

---

# HEAD AND EYES

Natural eye movement.

Occasional blinking.

Small eye refocusing.

Subtle head turns.

Micro-adjustments typical of real people.

Never stare continuously into the camera.

---

# HAIR

Natural secondary motion.

Hair reacts gently to body movement.

No excessive movement.

No floating strands.

No unrealistic simulation.

---

# FABRIC PHYSICS

Generate realistic clothing behavior.

Include:

* Natural folds
* Gravity
* Compression
* Fabric tension
* Slight stretching where appropriate
* Realistic draping

Avoid:

* Clipping
* Floating fabric
* Stiff cloth
* Unrealistic deformation

---

# HUMAN MOTION

Movement should exhibit authentic human biomechanics.

Include:

* Natural weight transfer
* Slight body sway
* Breathing motion
* Small balance corrections
* Organic timing
* Continuous motion

Avoid:

* Robotic timing
* Mechanical movement
* Looping animations
* Frozen poses
* Sudden acceleration
* Sudden stops

---

# SKIN PRESERVATION

Preserve visible skin exactly.

If previously hidden skin becomes visible during motion:

* Match the existing skin tone.
* Match the existing skin texture.

Do not generate:

* Tattoos
* Piercings
* Body paint
* Scars
* Moles
* Freckles
* Skin markings
* Decorative elements

Previously unseen skin must remain clean and consistent with the visible skin.

---

# VISUAL QUALITY

Hyper-realistic.

Real smartphone video appearance.

Natural motion blur.

Accurate lighting.

Realistic shadows.

Professional image quality.

No CGI appearance.

No AI artifacts.

---

# RESTRICTIONS

No audio.

No speech.

No music.

No ambient sound.

No subtitles.

No captions.

No text.

No logos.

No UI.

No filters.

No cinematic effects.

No stylization.

No dance choreography.

No exaggerated posing.

---

# FINAL RESULT

Produce a hyper-realistic silent POV fashion presentation in which the subject appears completely alive and natural.

The movements, facial expressions, breathing, posture, clothing behavior, and eye movements should be indistinguishable from those of a real person casually recording a short smartphone video while naturally presenting her outfit.
