# REALISTIC POV FASHION PRESENTATION

## OBJECTIVE

Animate the subject from the input image as a real person naturally presenting her outfit in a realistic POV-style video.

Preserve the subject's identity exactly.

The result should look like an authentic smartphone recording rather than a staged fashion shoot.

---

# IDENTITY LOCK

Preserve exactly:

* Face
* Facial identity
* Hair
* Skin tone
* Skin texture
* Makeup
* Body shape
* Body proportions
* Clothing
* Accessories
* Pose
* Expression

Identity preservation has absolute priority.

Do NOT beautify, redesign, stylize, retouch, or reinterpret any part of the subject.

---

# CAMERA

POV perspective from a person standing still while holding a smartphone.

The camera remains intentionally steady throughout the video.

Maintain:

* Chest-to-face height
* Fixed framing
* Fixed focal length
* No zoom
* No pan
* No tilt
* No handheld shake
* No micro-jitter
* No focus breathing

The subject moves naturally while the camera never moves.

---

# ANIMATION SEQUENCE

The animation flows continuously without pauses or abrupt transitions.

### 1. Initial Position

The subject begins standing comfortably at a natural distance from the camera.

She appears relaxed and comfortable.

---

### 2. Approach

She calmly walks toward the camera with two small, natural steps.

Her movement is relaxed and confident.

No exaggerated modeling or posing.

---

### 3. Clothing Detail

While approaching, she gently raises one hand and lightly touches the bow or lace detail on the chest.

She briefly holds the fabric.

The gesture feels spontaneous, delicate and natural, as if casually showing a clothing detail.

No emphasis.

No dramatic presentation.

After approximately one second, she naturally releases the fabric.

---

### 4. Return

She calmly takes one small step backward, returning close to her original position.

Movement remains fluid and continuous.

---

### 5. Natural Hair Adjustment

After returning to a relaxed stance:

She casually lifts one hand.

She gently brushes a few strands of hair behind the side of her head.

The gesture lasts approximately one second.

It feels completely unconscious and natural, like a brief self-adjustment.

The hand immediately returns to a relaxed resting position.

The gesture happens only once.

No posing.

No emphasis.

---

### 6. Clothing Presentation

She makes small, natural movements that subtly reveal the outfit.

Examples include:

* Slight torso rotation
* Gentle weight shifts
* Relaxed posture adjustments

The clothing remains the visual focus.
